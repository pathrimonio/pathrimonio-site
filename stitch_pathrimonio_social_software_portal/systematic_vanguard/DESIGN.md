## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a balance between high-performance software engineering and systemic social impact. The brand archetype is the visionary architect: technical, disciplined, rigorous, yet purposefully directed toward long-term human progress.

The visual style blends **High-Precision Dark Minimalism** with **Subtle Glassmorphism**. Deep carbon tones create an immersive, low-fatigue workspace that lets data density, structural clarity, and high-impact metrics stand out. Crisp geometric layouts, subtle luminescent borders, and deliberate amber-gold accents convey forward momentum, algorithmic rigor, and institutional solidity.

## Colors

The palette is engineered specifically for deep-contrast environments:

- **Primary Accent (`#F5A623`)**: The kinetic amber drawn from the brand identity. It signifies upward velocity, high-priority state transitions, active indicators, and actionable impact metrics.
- **Secondary / High-Contrast Highlight (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure crisp white for primary headlines, structural vector lines, and primary callout icons, ensuring maximum legibility against dark planes.
- **Tertiary Surface Layer (`#17171A`)**: Charcoal surface base used for cards, floating consoles, and elevation steps, differentiating modules from the background canvas.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0B0B0C`)**: An ultra-deep black base tone that eliminates eye fatigue and serves as the grounding plane for all systemic UI modules.
- **Functional Alpha Overlays**: Subtle perimeter boundaries utilize white with 8% to 12% opacity (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), preventing visual harshness while maintaining crisp geometry.

## Typography

The pairing creates a dialogue between technological authority and human readability:

- **Display & Headings (`Space Grotesk`)**: Provides an engineering-grade, geometric cadence. Its distinct alphanumeric shapes reflect technical precision, modular architecture, and structured rigor.
- **Body & Continuous Reading (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers clear readability on dark backgrounds with balanced counters and humanist warmth, preventing technological coldness during long analytical sessions.
- **Labels & Micro-data (`Space Grotesk`)**: Uppercase-ready tracking and deliberate proportioning ensure metric figures, status trackers, and badges remain legible at condensed sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt architectural rhythm, utilizing a fluid 12-column grid system for desktop and tablet screens, folding down to a 4-column system on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12 columns with 24px gutters and generous outer margins (48px) to anchor high-density dashboard modules and technical narrative panels.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with 20px gutters and 32px outer canvas bounds.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with 16px gutters and 20px outer margins. Metric cards reflow from multi-column grids into vertical command stacks.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than conventional drop shadows that blur into obscurity against deep black, elevation is achieved via **luminous surface layering and low-contrast outlines**:

1. **Base Layer (Canvas)**: Solid `#0B0B0C`.
2. **Level 1 (Cards, Modules, Structural Panels)**: Background `#17171A` with a 1px perimeter stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
3. **Level 2 (Active Metric Tiles & Glassmorphic Sheets)**: Background `rgba(23, 23, 26, 0.75)` with a 16px backdrop-filter blur and top-edge accent highlight `rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.25)`.
4. **Level 3 (Modals, Command Palettes, Popovers)**: Solid `#1C1C21` bordered by `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)` and cast with an ambient directional glow: `0px 20px 48px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0px 0px 24px 0px rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.05)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric standard. Corner radiuses follow precise mathematical constraints:
- Base elements (buttons, inputs, status tags): `0.25rem` (4px).
- Containers & interactive cards: `0.5rem` (8px).
- Major structural panels & dashboards: `0.75rem` (12px).

This subtle curvature softens technical density without slipping into casual or playful aesthetics, maintaining the discipline of engineering infrastructure.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Kinetic**: Solid `#F5A623` fill with bold `#0B0B0C` typography. On hover, expands with a soft perimeter halo `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.35)`.
- **Secondary Ghost**: Transparent background with a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` and white typography. Shifts to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` fill on hover.
- **Engineering Action**: Dark charcoal `#17171A` surface with an amber left-accent bar (2px width) indicating active workflow hooks.

### Metric Cards & Impact Panels
- Constructed on `#17171A` with a hairline stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- Metric numerals are rendered in `Space Grotesk` with high visual weight, accompanied by directional delta arrows (inspired by the upward angle of the brand mark).
- Top-right corner anchors an amber status badge indicating real-time system performance or verified social impact metrics.

### Badges & Chips
- **Social Impact Badges**: Monospaced-styled `Space Grotesk` labels set in uppercase with a 10% amber fill (`rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.1)`), an amber perimeter border, and `#F5A623` text.
- **System Telemetry Chips**: Neutral charcoal fill (`#222227`) with a 6px glowing indicator dot (green for active deployment, amber for optimization needed).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs**: Background `#0F0F12` framed by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. When focused, transitions to a crisp `#F5A623` border with `0 0 0 1px #F5A623` ring. Placeholder text is muted carbon (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom square components (4px corner radius) using `#17171A` background; checked state fills with `#F5A623` featuring an ultra-dark vector checkmark.

### Data Lists & Code Vectors
- Alternating subtle row divisions (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`). Row hover activates a subtle `rgba(245, 166, 35, 0.03)` highlight with immediate pointer feedback.
